# Playground

The frame for viewing this design system: navigation, a canvas, and sometimes an inspector.

## Language

**Playground**:
The frame used to view the design system, made of navigation, a canvas, and sometimes an inspector.
_Avoid_: Catalog, docs site, detail page

**Welcome**:
The home canvas, a small arrangement of live components.
_Avoid_: Index, directory, landing page

**Component page**:
A page devoted to one component.
_Avoid_: Detail page, specimen page

**Foundation page**:
A page devoted to one foundation, such as color or size.
_Avoid_: Token page

**Canvas**:
The center pane of the playground.
_Avoid_: Preview, stage

**Gallery**:
Every option of a component, shown at once.
_Avoid_: All options, call options, catalog

**Specimen**:
The single instance of a component that the inspector drives.
_Avoid_: Preview, example

**Inspector**:
The pane of live controls, a props list, and a composition note for the specimen.
_Avoid_: Props panel, controls, sidebar
